Why A80 exists: spi/core.c:spi_transfer_wait() budgets `2*(8000*len/speed_hz)+200 ms` and trusts
spi-max-frequency=9600000, but with force_pio the QUP SE FIFO (fifo_bytes=64) only moves ~250 KB/s.
The vendor NVT_FW_TRANSFER_MAX of 63 KiB puts 64513 B on the wire: 307 ms of budget against ~310 ms of
PIO, so the download dies about half the time with "SPI transfer timed out" -> error -ETIMEDOUT ->
"firmware SRAM transfer failed", probe returns without an input device (A76/A78/A79 measured it, A77
got lucky).  16 KiB chunks need ~65 ms against a 227 ms budget; A79 proved the full chain then
completes from userspace (`error=0`, both CRC snapshots equal, `firmware running: version 0x1d,
PID 0x6066, 16000x25600`, `input: NT3652N Capacitive Touchscreen`, 30833 events).
Why force_pio exists: a transfer larger than the SE FIFO makes geni_can_dma() answer yes, transfer_one()
picks GENI_SE_DMA, and the board resets (A71/A72 lost a whole boot that way; A74 confirmed both
directions in one boot: PIO -> CRC equal and events; DMA -> last card step is dma-stage3 and silence).
Why the firmware is in the initramfs: the driver is built-in, so its probe runs during initcalls while
the root is still the embedded initramfs; a blob in /lib/firmware on the UFS root is invisible then
(A75: boot-nvt-begin=0).  A76 put novatek/lenovo-tb320fc.bin (196608 B, sha256 d08d4844...d4dad3) at
